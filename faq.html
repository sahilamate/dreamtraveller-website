Help & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about booking procedures, advance tokens, cancellation refunds, and customized travel.

How do I book a tour package with Dream Travelkar?

You can book directly through our website by clicking the "Book Now" button on any package card. You can secure your dates by paying a nominal 30% advance token (demonstrated on this frontend portal). Alternatively, submit an enquiry or call our Worli, Mumbai office (+91 9867807837) for customized family departures.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

For domestic tours (Rajasthan, Kerala, Himachal, Konkan), cancellations made up to 15 days before the departure date receive a full 100% refund. Cancellations between 7–14 days prior receive a 50% refund. Within 7 days, standard hotel holding charges apply. International flight and ferry tickets follow respective carrier terms.

Are Indian and vegetarian food options guaranteed on international tours?

Yes, absolutely! On all our international departures (Thailand, Dubai, Bali, Switzerland), we coordinate with verified Indian restaurants and chef-supervised kitchens to ensure authentic North Indian, South Indian, Pure Vegetarian, and Jain meal preferences are accommodated.

What is the special Maharashtra heritage circuit?

Our Maharashtra Heritage circuit is Dream Travelkar's signature historical travel initiative honoring the legacy of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. We conduct special guided expeditions to coastal naval fortresses like Sindhudurg, Vijaydurg, Suvarnadurg, as well as mountain forts (Raigad, Rajgad, Pratapgad) with expert historians and cultural storytellers.

Do you provide customized pickup for families from Mumbai & Pune?

Yes. For private customized holidays and small group tours, we provide door-to-door or central hub pickups (Dadar, Borivali, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Vashi, and Pune) in comfortable air-conditioned vehicles (Innova Crysta, Urbania, and Tempo Travelers).

How do I access my booking voucher or invoice?

You can log in to our Customer Dashboard anytime using your email address to view all active bookings, simulated payment transaction receipts, and print your official travel booking voucher with a single click.
