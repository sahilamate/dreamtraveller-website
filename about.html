Our Founding Vision

Making Extraordinary Travel Enriching & Accessible

Beautiful dream begins with dreamers, and the journey to make that beautiful dream come true begins with Dream Travelkar. Headquartered in Worli, Mumbai, our mission is to make it possible for families, working professionals, senior citizens, and young travelers to explore the magnificent wonders of the world by making tourism reliable and affordable.

We arrange innovative guided group tours, specialty cultural circuits, and customized holidays suitable for all ages with the core objective of delivering a meaningful, memorable, and valuable travel experience. It is reassuring to know you are in the hands of professionals who have truly been there and genuinely understand traveler needs.
